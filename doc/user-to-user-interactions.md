# User-to-User Interaction Brainstorm

## Purpose

Manoa Bathroom Finder can let users share restroom information and respond to each other’s contributions. These interactions help the UH Mānoa community find available, usable restrooms.

## Ideas

### 1. Community Restroom Status Updates
Users can report “All stalls occupied,” “Some stalls available,” or “Available now.” Reports show a timestamp and expire after 10 minutes. Other users can confirm or update the status. Reports do not identify who is using the restroom.

### 2. Supply Updates
Users can report missing toilet paper, soap, or paper towels. Other users can confirm the shortage or mark supplies as restocked.

### 3. Condition Alerts
Users can report clogged toilets, broken locks, wet floors, or temporary closures. Others can confirm that a problem remains or mark it resolved.

### 4. Helpful Votes and Review Reactions
Users can mark reviews as helpful, agree or disagree with an observation, or thank someone for a useful contribution.

### 5. Review Replies
Users can reply to reviews to ask questions, add details, or provide updated information.

### 6. Community Questions and Answers
Users can ask questions such as “Is this restroom open after 6?” Other users can answer based on their experience.

### 7. Accessibility Questions
Users can ask about entrances, elevators, and stall space. Other users can share observations, with community responses clearly distinguished from verified accessibility information.

### 8. Confirmations and Corrections
Users can mark a report “Still accurate” or “Resolved,” or suggest corrections to information another user submitted.

### 9. Follow a Restroom
Users can follow a restroom to receive notifications when someone reports a closure, supply shortage, or reopening.

### 10. Nearby Alternatives
Users can suggest nearby restrooms when a location is busy, closed, or temporarily unusable.

### 11. Community Tips and Recommendations
Users can share tips about shorter lines, quieter locations, privacy, or how to find a restroom entrance.

### 12. Photo Contributions
Users can contribute photos of entrances and signs to help others locate restrooms. Photos should exclude people and private restroom areas.

### 13. Community Confirmation Requests
Users can ask someone nearby to confirm whether a restroom is open or whether a reported problem has been resolved.

## Recommended Starting Feature

Start with community restroom status updates and confirmations. One user posts a temporary availability report, and another confirms or updates it. This provides a clear user-to-user interaction and directly supports the app’s purpose.

Busy reports should expire automatically because availability changes quickly. Maintenance and supply reports should remain separate and display their latest confirmation time.

## Scope

This document proposes ideas for team discussion. Implementing these features is separate from completing the brainstorming issue.
